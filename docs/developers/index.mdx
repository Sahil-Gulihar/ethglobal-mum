---
title: Accept USDT on Whitechain
description: A non-custodial USDT checkout. Customers pay from their own wallet straight to yours, you get a signed webhook when it settles, and you can refund from the same wallet.
---

Your server creates a checkout session, the customer pays from their wallet straight to your payout wallet on Whitechain Sepolia (chain id `1874`), and you get a signed webhook once the payment is confirmed onchain. Whitechain Commerce never holds your funds.
